# InkPreview — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
People exploring a tattoo idea — GenZ-leaning consumers who want to *visualize a
design on their own body* before committing, often impulsively on a phone; plus
desktop users browsing and designing more deliberately. Studios (B2B) later. The
job-to-be-done: go from a vague idea to "I can see this on me, and I want it" in
under a minute, then hand a clean file to an artist.

### Brand Personality
Bold · magical · confident. Voice is hyped but tasteful, a little irreverent
(GenZ) — never corporate. The interface should evoke excitement, confidence, and
anticipation ("I can't wait to get this inked").

### Aesthetic Direction — "Night Parlour" (2026 redesign, replaces "Acid Ink")
A tattoo parlour after dark: a lacquer-black wall, flash sheets pinned to it, one
neon sign in the window. Every visual choice comes from the shop itself:

- **Wall** (the UI ground) is warm lacquer black — never blue-black.
- **Flash paper** carries the work: every design sits on an off-white paper card
  (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`, so the generated white background becomes paper), taped
  to the wall, numbered like a flash sheet (`№ 417 · FINE LINE`). The consultation form
  and the export "artist ticket" are paper too — paper = the work, black = the shop.
- **One neon** (red-pink) is the shop-window sign: the primary action and the single
  hero word. A disabled CTA is an *unlit tube*; it flickers on when it's ready.
- **Stencil violet** belongs only to placement: the design is shown on the body photo
  as a purple thermal-stencil transfer (SVG filter), exactly what happens in a real shop
  before the needle. Stencil → ink is the story of the try-on step.
- **Flash gold / jade** are tiny highlights (stars, "most popular", success), never fills.
- Terms of art are content, not decoration: needle groupings for line weight
  (`3RL / 7RL / 11RL`), flash numbers, the shop process (sketch → stencil → ink),
  `PNG · 2048 px · 300 DPI`.

**Anti-references:** acid-lime-on-black rave UI, rainbow gradient text, glassmorphism,
emoji/sparkle markers on every label, cream+serif+terracotta "editorial" pages, generic
SaaS cards with the same radius and glow on everything.

### Tokens (frontend/src/styles/theme.css)
- Wall: `ground #0f0d0c`, `wall #141110`, `surface #1b1715`, `raised #25201d`,
  `line #362f2a`, `line-strong #4a413a`.
- Text on wall: `text #f5efe6`, `text-2 #c9beb3`, `text-3 #9c9086` (≥ 4.5:1 on ground and surface).
- Paper: `paper #f1e8d8`, `paper-2 #e5d9c3`, `paper-line #cbbda5`, `paper-ink #1c1714`,
  `paper-mute #5f5046` (≥ 4.5:1 on paper).
- Neon: `neon #ff3d57` (text on it: `neon-ink #22060b`), `neon-hi #ff6b7f`, `neon-core #ffe0e4`.
- Stencil: `stencil #8a6cff` (on wall), `stencil-ink #4a2fb2` (on paper/skin).
- Flash: `gold #f3b93b`, `jade #3fa36f`.

### Type
- **Archivo** (variable, width axis) — body at 100% width; headings in *condensed caps*
  (`font-stretch ~70%`, 800) like shop signage.
- **Grenze Gotisch** — blackletter, only for single words / short phrases (the neon hero
  word, "ready to ink", 404, the footer tagline). Never body copy.
- **Courier Prime** — typewriter labels: flash numbers, meta, form labels, prompts
  quoted back. Uppercase + tracking for labels.
- Fonts are self-hosted in `frontend/public/fonts/` (no Google Fonts requests — GDPR),
  shared by the app and the Worker's SEO pages.

### Design Principles
1. **The tattoo is the hero.** Real generated designs on flash paper; never abstract filler.
2. **One neon per view.** Neon marks the next action or the one hero word — nothing else glows.
3. **Materials, not effects.** Paper, tape, stencil, ticket perforations, a lit/unlit tube.
   No blurred color blobs, no gradient text.
4. **Two real layouts.** Thumb-first mobile (docked bottom bar, compact) and an editorial
   desktop (two-column hero, big condensed type, flash wall) — not one scaled to the other.
5. **Content visible at rest.** Nothing waits at `opacity: 0` for a scroll observer; motion
   only settles things into place and always honors `prefers-reduced-motion`.
6. **Impeccable craft.** Deliberate type scale, aligned edges, visible focus, AA contrast.
